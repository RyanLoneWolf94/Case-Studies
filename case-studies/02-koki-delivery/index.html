01 · The Brief

One platform, three hard problems at once

Koki Technologies runs a cross-border e-commerce and logistics marketplace connecting Chinese manufacturers with consumers in Zimbabwe and South Africa. It has to do three difficult things simultaneously: run a multi-vendor storefront, consolidate international freight through warehouse depots in China, and settle payments across African mobile-money rails most off-the-shelf systems have never heard of.

The engagement started with building the initial web e-commerce system — and grew into something bigger. Today I'm Koki's lead developer and DevOps engineer across the full ecosystem: web, mobile app, server infrastructure, payments, and app-store presence.

Keep a live, revenue-generating cross-border platform stable, compliant, and evolving — while re-architecting the pieces holding it back.

03 · What I Built & Took Over

From a build to full ownership

💳

Payment gateway integration

A cross-border payment pipeline tuned for the Zimbabwean market, where card-only checkout fails most buyers. Paynow integration for real-time EcoCash, OneMoney, Innbucks and Zimswitch — plus an offline receipt / QR audit flow that locks orders in "Pending Audit" until staff verify an uploaded bank screenshot.

🎨

UI revamp

Modernised the storefront and Uni-App mobile shell — tightening the in-app navigation and fixing the webview-embed pattern so custom tools (freight calculator, custom pages) surface cleanly inside the native app without breaking Uni-App routing.

🔔

Notification system

Rebuilt the seller order-notification system after sellers stopped getting order emails. Root-caused it to a downed background queue worker — buyer inquiries fired synchronously and kept working, which was the diagnostic key — then fixed and hardened it to survive reboots.

🛠️

DevOps & infrastructure

Single point of accountability for live infrastructure: EIP and DNS management, full-stack restart sequencing, firewall and access control, and documented runbooks so the platform stays recoverable and maintainable.

Incident · INC-2026-051

Full platform blackout — recovered

A hosting-account billing freeze triggered a hard shutdown on Huawei Cloud and released the server's Elastic IP, cutting it off from the public internet entirely. I ran the complete recovery: re-provisioned a fresh EIP and rebound it, restored firewall/SSH access, brought the full stack back up in the correct order (MySQL → PHP-FPM → Nginx → queue worker → cron), and realigned the Aliyun DNS to the new IP to restore the domain.
