## 1. The Client & The Brief

Tiny Giants Academy is a LoneWolf Digital client in the early-childhood space. The
engagement is **event branding** for the school's two headline occasions of the year — the
**Annual Fiesta** and the **ECD-B Graduation** — beginning with the event **invite**.

**The core challenge:** give a children's academy event a look that feels celebratory and
polished while staying warm, age-appropriate, and unmistakably Tiny Giants.

---

## 2. Scope

Event-branding work tracked under the reference prefix **TIN-EVT**, starting with the
**Annual Fiesta & ECD-B Graduation invite** as the first deliverable, with the broader
event identity following from it.
