## 1. The Client & The Brief

WYFIX is a Harare-based company in **construction and chemical products** — a technical,
industrial category where trust, durability, and professionalism carry the buying
decision. The brief was a **full brand system**: not a logo in isolation, but the complete
scaffolding a company needs to present itself consistently across industrial, retail, and
corporate touchpoints.

**The core challenge:** give a products-and-trades business a brand that reads as
**credible and industrial-grade** while staying clean, modern, and easy to apply across
packaging, documents, and digital surfaces.

---

## 2. Discovery & Strategy

The foundation was a written **brand strategy** and a **Brand DNA** document — positioning,
attributes, and the rationale that governs every downstream craft decision. These sit at
the top of the system so that colour, type, and layout choices are traceable to strategy
rather than taste.

---

## 3. Visual Identity

### Colour system
A disciplined industrial palette anchored by a single, constant brand accent.

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Teal | `#0C3B45` |
| Brand constant (accent) | Orange | `#FE5F04` |
| Supporting | Navy | `#00295D` |
| Light | Off-white | `#F1EBED` |

The **orange is the brand constant** — the recognisable thread that carries across every
application, grounded against deep teal and navy for an industrial, trustworthy feel.

### Typography
| Use | Typeface |
|---|---|
| Display / headers | **Barlow Condensed** |
| Body | **Barlow** |

A condensed display face paired with its regular-width sibling keeps headlines tight and
strong while body copy stays highly legible — a practical pairing for a technical brand.

### Logo & applications
A documented logo system with **letterhead** and brand applications, plus **packaging
mockups** reviewed as concept/family designs for sign-off (concept approval, not
production artwork).

## 5. Process — Client Interaction to Delivery

```
Brand strategy & DNA  →  Colour & type system  →  Logo documentation  →  Letterhead & applications
   →  Ad scripts & PPTX storyboard  →  Packaging concept mockups (sign-off)
   →  Internal workflow dashboard  →  Client-facing handover portal  →  Case study page
```

The engagement paired **written strategy** with **production-grade design** and wrapped
delivery in **two HTML dashboards** — one to run the project internally, one to hand the
finished kit to the client with a clean download experience. Packaging was presented as
concept/family designs for approval rather than production files, keeping the sign-off
focused on direction.

---

## 6. Outcome

WYFIX moved from a products business to a **fully-systematised brand** with strategy, DNA,
a complete and documented visual identity, rollout-ready applications, and a client portal
for handover. The system was built to be **applied and extended** — consistent across the
industrial, retail, and corporate surfaces a construction-and-chemicals company has to
operate on — and documented as a repeatable LoneWolf brand-system engagement.
